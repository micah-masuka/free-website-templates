Getting there

By car: All Saints is on Samora Machel Avenue near Fourth Street. Celebration Centre is in Borrowdale — follow signs to Swan Drive. Shuttle departs the church forecourt at 3:30 PM.

From out of town: Most guests fly into Robert Gabriel Mugabe International Airport. Taxis and ride-hail are available — allow 45–60 minutes to Borrowdale in Saturday traffic.
