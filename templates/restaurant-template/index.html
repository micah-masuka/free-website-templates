The kitchen

Chef Ana Reyes opened Ember & Oak in 2019 after five years over open fire in Cape Town. The menu follows what arrives from farms in Mashonaland and what her crew can pull from a 900° oak hearth.

Forty-two seats inside, sixteen on the patio. Walk-ins at the bar after 8 PM on weeknights.
