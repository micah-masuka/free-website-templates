Your clipboard was never meant for a job site.

Relay is a pocket-sized field recorder that captures readings, photos, and voice notes — then syncs to your ops dashboard when you are back in coverage. Built for ZESA field crews, municipal water teams, and inspection contractors who still lose half a day to paperwork.

01

The problem

Technicians write readings on paper, photograph meters with personal phones, and text supervisors numbers that never reach the work order. Office staff re-type everything. Relay keeps one record tied to the job ID from the truck.

Questions

Does Relay replace our CMMS?

No. Relay feeds data into systems you already use — ServiceTitan, Fiix, or a spreadsheet if that's where you are today.

What does it cost?

Pilot pricing is shared on the waitlist. Expect hardware plus a monthly sync fee per active device — no per-seat tax.

Who is this not for?

Desk-only teams, one-off residential jobs without repeat assets, or orgs that need full ERP in the field.
