Operational & financial restructuring

We work with leadership teams when performance has outgrown the organisation that produces it.

Vantage advises private-equity-backed industrials and family-owned distributors through carve-outs, margin recovery, and post-merger integration. Engagements are partner-led, fixed in scope, and documented to board standards.

Request a confidential briefing →

Practices

  1. 01

    Margin architecture

    SKU rationalisation, freight and procurement diagnostics, pricing governance. Typical entry: 12-week diagnostic with board-ready findings.

  2. 02

    Carve-out & integration

    Day-one operating models, TSA exit planning, standalone finance and HR build. We sit in the deal team room, not a satellite office.

  3. 03

    Working-capital release

    Inventory, receivables, and payables programmes tied to cash conversion metrics the CFO already reports.

  4. 04

    Interim leadership

    Operating partner placements during CEO or COO transitions — usually 6–12 months with explicit exit criteria.

Approach

Every engagement begins with a two-week diagnostic. We interview functional heads, review three years of segment data, and walk two facilities or sites. You receive a written assessment — not a slide deck — with ranked interventions and implementation cost ranges.

We do not staff projects with recent graduates billed as principals. Two named partners remain accountable through close-out.

Phase Duration Deliverable
Diagnostic 2–3 weeks Written assessment, board summary
Design 4–8 weeks Target operating model, implementation plan
Execution support 3–9 months Embedded partner, weekly steering
